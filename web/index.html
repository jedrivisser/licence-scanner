SA Licence Scanner

Reads the barcode on the back of a South African driving licence card, entirely in your browser.

Scanner Shows the details and photo stored in a licence card's barcode. Licence Barcode Keeps your own licence's barcode on your phone and shows it full screen. Not a substitute for your licence card.

Privacy

Everything happens on your device. The camera image and the licence's details are never uploaded, and there are no analytics, ads or cookies.

The scanner keeps nothing once you close it, except which of your cameras to use. The Licence Barcode app keeps the scanned barcode in this browser's storage on this device only; remove it with the bin button next to the barcode, or by uninstalling the app or clearing this site's data.

The site is served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare. Like any website, they may log basic request details (such as IP address and time) when a page loads. Those requests never contain licence data.

If you scan someone else's licence, you are responsible for handling their personal information lawfully (in South Africa, under POPIA): have a reason, ask them first, and don't keep copies you don't need.

Not an official service

This is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Transport, the RTMC, the eNaTIS system or any other government body.

It shows what the barcode contains. A barcode that reads correctly doesn't prove that the card is genuine, that the licence is valid or that the person showing it is its holder.

The Licence Barcode app is not a driving licence. The law requires drivers to carry their licence card and produce it when asked by a traffic officer; a barcode on a phone doesn't replace it.
